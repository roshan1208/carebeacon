HIQA (the Health Information and Quality Authority) registers and inspects Ireland's nursing homes as designated centres for older people. It does not rate or grade them, so no quality badge is shown here. Its inspection reports are published per centre on hiqa.ie. What this page shows is the register itself, and what changed in it.
