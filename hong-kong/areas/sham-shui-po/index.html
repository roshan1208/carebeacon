Residential care homes for the elderly in Sham Shui Po

93 residential care homes for the elderly on Social Welfare Department's register. Register as published 23 September 2026.

Hong Kong's Social Welfare Department licenses residential care homes for the elderly and publishes the list daily. It does not grade them. Two facts on its list are shown as flags: whether a home is accredited under the Residential Care Homes accreditation scheme, and whether it has a conviction record under the Residential Care Homes (Elderly Persons) Ordinance. Homes in Guangdong Province are those in the SWD's Residential Care Services Scheme in Guangdong.

Homes with a Social Welfare Department licence number are matched between versions on that number. The homes in the Guangdong scheme carry no licence number in the file, so those are matched by name and district, and by address where a name changes. Where the address changes too, a renamed home appears here as one leaving the list and another joining it.
