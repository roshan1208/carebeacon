Residential care homes for the elderly in Kwai Tsing

88 residential care homes for the elderly on Social Welfare Department's register. Register as published 14 September 2026.

Hong Kong's Social Welfare Department licenses residential care homes for the elderly and publishes the list daily. It does not grade them. Two facts on its list are shown as flags: whether a home is accredited under the Residential Care Homes accreditation scheme, and whether it has a conviction record under the Residential Care Homes (Elderly Persons) Ordinance. Homes in Guangdong Province are those in the SWD's Residential Care Services Scheme in Guangdong.
