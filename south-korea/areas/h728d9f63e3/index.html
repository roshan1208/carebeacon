Residential long-term care facilities in 강원특별자치도

346 residential long-term care facilities on National Health Insurance Service (NHIS)'s register. Register as published 14 September 2026.

South Korea's National Health Insurance Service evaluates long-term care institutions on a roughly three-year cycle and grades them A (highest) to E (lowest), with a total score out of 100. The grade shown is the NHIS's own most recent published grade for the facility. Facilities that have not yet been evaluated do not appear in the NHIS file and so are not on these pages.
