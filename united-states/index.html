Nursing homes in the United States

14,690 nursing homes on CMS (Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services)'s register, across 53 states. Register as published 14 September 2026 (CMS refreshes Care Compare monthly). · Operators

CMS rates every Medicare- and Medicaid-certified nursing home from 1 to 5 stars overall, combining health inspections, staffing and quality measures, each also rated 1 to 5. The stars shown are CMS's own published ratings. A Special Focus Facility is one CMS has placed under increased scrutiny for a persistent record of serious problems; the abuse icon marks a home cited for abuse or neglect.
