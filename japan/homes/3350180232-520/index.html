介護老人保健施設　はるか

岡山県岡山市東区西大寺北　941, 岡山市東区

Japan's Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare publishes the register of long-term care insurance facilities twice a year. Three residential types are shown: special nursing homes for the elderly (介護老人福祉施設), geriatric health services facilities (介護老人保健施設) and long-term care medical facilities (介護医療院). The Ministry publishes no quality rating; capacity (定員) is optional in the disclosure and is shown only where given.
