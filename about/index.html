How CareBeacon works

Last data update 14 September 2026. Page generated 14 September 2026.

What this site is

The regulators of care homes in the UK publish today's picture: the current rating or grade, the current registered manager, whether a home is registered. They do not publish what changed. CareBeacon keeps the picture from each update and shows the differences — a home downgraded, a home that lost its registration, a new manager, a new inspection — so that a family, a council commissioner or an adviser can see movement, not just a snapshot. Everything here is derived from the regulators' own published data.

Sources, by country

Every country covered, and every country not yet covered with the reason.

  • England15,335 registered care homes from the Care Quality Commission's Syndication API, checked daily. CQC rates on a four-point scale — Outstanding, Good, Requires improvement, Inadequate — overall and for five key questions. Changes shown: rating up or down, first rating, registration lost, registration regained. The public change window on this site is 90 days; 1,724 operator pages cover groups running two or more homes.
  • Scotland1,380 registered care homes from the Care Inspectorate's monthly datastore (latest snapshot 31 August 2026). The Care Inspectorate grades 1 (Unsatisfactory) to 6 (Excellent) on up to five quality themes; the grade shown for a home is its lowest current theme grade, which is how the Care Inspectorate itself summarises a service. Changes shown: grade up or down, first grading, new registration, cancellation. Because the Care Inspectorate keeps an archive, each Scottish home's page carries its grade history back to 31 March 2015 — annual to 2025, monthly since — and a change in an annual window is shown as such, never as a day the source did not give.
  • Northern Ireland468 registered nursing and residential care homes from RQIA's register on Open Data NI (latest file 3 August 2026, updated roughly monthly). RQIA inspects and reports but does not rate or grade, so no quality badge is shown. Changes shown: new registration, cancellation, a change of registered manager, a new inspection. There is no archive, so history begins with the first file this site recorded.
  • Wales1,480 registered care home services from Care Inspectorate Wales's directory export (latest 14 September 2026; CIW refreshes it daily). CIW rates four themes — well-being, care and support, environment, leadership and management — as Excellent, Good, Requires Improvement or Requires Significant Improvement, and publishes no overall rating; where one grade appears here it is the lowest of the four themes, labelled as our summary. Changes shown: a theme rating up or down, first rating, new registration, a service leaving the register, a change of responsible individual. Ratings began in April 2025, so many services are not yet rated. There is no archive; history begins with the first export this site recorded.
  • Ireland546 designated centres for older people from HIQA's register export (latest 14 September 2026; checked daily). HIQA inspects and publishes reports but does not rate or grade, so no quality badge is shown. Changes shown: new registration, cancellation, a change in the conditions of registration, a change of person in charge, a change in registered places, a renewed registration, a Chief Inspector's note (so far, notice of intention to close). There is no archive; history begins with the first register this site recorded.

Update cadence

England is refreshed every day from CQC's change feed, Wales every day from CIW's directory export, and Ireland every day from HIQA's register export. Scotland and Northern Ireland are refreshed the day a new register file appears, which is roughly monthly. Every page states the date of the data it shows.

What we do not do

  • We do not inspect, rate or recommend care homes. Ratings and grades are the regulators' own published judgements, reproduced as published.
  • We do not collect reviews, prices or availability.
  • We do not hold personal data about residents. Registered managers' names appear only where the regulator publishes them.
  • A change on this site is the difference between two of a regulator's published states. If the regulator corrects its data, the change here may be a correction, not an event.
  • Distances on English home pages are straight-line, not by road.

Licences and independence

All source data is reused under the Open Government Licence v3.0. CareBeacon is independent of, and not endorsed by, the Care Quality Commission, the Care Inspectorate, CIW, RQIA, HIQA or any care provider. Irish data is re-used under Ireland's Public Sector Information regulations, as HIQA's site permits. If something here looks wrong, the regulator's own record — linked from every home's page — is the authority.
