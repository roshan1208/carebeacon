Woonzorgcentra (residential care homes) in Antwerp

203 woonzorgcentra (residential care homes) on Departement Zorg (Flemish Department of Care)'s register. Register as published 15 September 2026.

The Flemish Department of Care recognises and inspects woonzorgcentra (residential care homes for older people) in Flanders and publishes this register. It does not grade them; inspection reports are published per home as PDFs. Resident counts and the average daily price are as the home last reported them to the Department; small counts are published as <5. Belgium regulates care homes regionally, so Wallonia and Brussels are not on these pages.
