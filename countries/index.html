Countries

5 countries, 19,229 registered care homes, each tracked against its own regulator's published register. Page generated 14 September 2026.

Every country keeps its own regulator's language and scale. Grades are never compared across countries, because they measure different things in different ways.

Covered

  • England Care Quality Commission (CQC)15,355 homes · Outstanding / Good / Requires improvement / Inadequate · tracks ratings up or down, first ratings, registrations lost and regained, manager gaps · data as of 14 September 2026
  • Scotland Care Inspectorate1,380 homes · grades 1 (Unsatisfactory) to 6 (Excellent); summarised by the lowest · tracks grade movements across five quality themes back to 2015, registrations, cancellations · data as of 31 August 2026
  • Wales Care Inspectorate Wales (CIW)1,480 homes · Excellent / Good / Requires Improvement / Requires Significant Improvement across four themes; no overall rating · tracks theme ratings up or down, first ratings, registrations, cancellations, responsible individuals · data as of 14 September 2026
  • Northern Ireland RQIA468 homes · RQIA does not rate or grade · tracks registrations, cancellations, registered manager changes, new inspections · data as of 3 August 2026
  • Ireland HIQA546 homes · HIQA does not rate or grade · tracks registrations, cancellations, conditions of registration, persons in charge, notices of intention to close · data as of 14 September 2026

Not yet covered, and why

Change tracking needs a regulator that publishes a per-home register in a machine-readable form under a licence that allows reuse. Where that exists, it is on the list to build. Where it does not, saying so is the most useful thing this page can do. Checked 14 September 2026.

  • United StatesCMS publishes a monthly register of ~14,700 nursing homes with 1–5 star ratings and a seven-year archive. In progress: the next country to be added.
  • AustraliaQuarterly Star Ratings extracts (1–5 stars) for ~2,600 aged care homes; the register itself is published only as a PDF. Planned.
  • New ZealandHealthCERT publishes a CSV of certified aged care premises with certification periods. Planned.
  • Hong KongThe Social Welfare Department publishes a daily XML list of 1,100 licensed residential care homes with accreditation and conviction flags. Planned.
  • South KoreaThe National Health Insurance Service publishes annual evaluation grades for long-term care institutions as open CSV. Planned.
  • TaiwanNursing homes and elderly welfare institutions are published as open CSV with evaluation results. Planned.
  • FranceFINESS lists every EHPAD daily under an open licence, but no quality ratings are published as data. Planned as a register without grades.
  • JapanThe care service information system publishes twice-yearly open CSVs of every establishment, with no quality fields. Planned as a register without grades.
  • BelgiumFlanders publishes a CSV of every woonzorgcentrum; Wallonia and Brussels publish lists; inspection reports are PDFs. Planned as a register without grades.
  • GermanyInspection grades exist but are published only inside insurers' portals; the register is available to third parties on request. No open dataset. Not covered.
  • SpainNo current national per-home register is published; the last open snapshot is a 2019 research dataset. No open dataset. Not covered.
  • ItalyNo national register of RSAs is published as data; a few regions publish lists. No open dataset. Not covered.
  • CanadaOntario publishes inspection reports per home as web pages and PDFs; other provinces vary; no national data. Would need scraping. Not covered.
  • India, South Africa, Brazil, SingaporeNo per-home register of care homes is published; only counts, or nothing. No public register exists. Not covered.
