FINESS is France's national register of health and social care establishments, maintained by the Agence du Numérique en Santé for the Ministry of Health. These pages show every active EHPAD (établissement d'hébergement pour personnes âgées dépendantes). France publishes no quality rating as open data, and this export carries no capacity figures; what is shown is the register itself and what changed in it.
