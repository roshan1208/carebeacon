EHPAD (care homes for dependent older people) in Pas-de-Calais

136 EHPAD (care homes for dependent older people) on FINESS (ANS, Ministry of Health)'s register. Register as published 21 September 2026.

FINESS is France's national register of health and social care establishments, maintained by the Agence du Numérique en Santé for the Ministry of Health. These pages show every active EHPAD (établissement d'hébergement pour personnes âgées dépendantes). France publishes no quality rating as open data and this export carries no capacity figures, so what is shown is the register itself and what changed in it — plus, for the homes that have declared them, the accommodation prices and dependency tariffs the CNSA publishes because the law requires every EHPAD to declare what it charges.
