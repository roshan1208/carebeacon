Aged residential care premises in Waikato District Health Board

59 aged residential care premises on HealthCERT (Ministry of Health)'s register. Register as published 21 September 2026.

HealthCERT (New Zealand's Ministry of Health) certifies aged residential care providers for 12, 24, 36 or 48 months after each audit; a longer period reflects a better audit outcome, and 48 months is the maximum. The period shown is the Ministry's own published figure. Where the file records no period for the current certificate, nothing is shown. Audit summaries are published per provider on the Ministry's site.

The Ministry's file carries no identifier for a premises, so each one is matched between versions by its name and town. Where a premises is renamed but keeps its street address, that is shown here as a new name rather than a closure. Where the address changes too, or two premises share one address, nothing in the file tells a rename apart from a real closure and a real opening, and both are shown.
