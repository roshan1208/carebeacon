138 aged care homes across 72 local government areas. Register as published 30 June 2025.

This is the Australian Government's own list of aged care services, published by the Australian Institute of Health and Welfare, showing every service with residential places as at 30 June. Australia does rate its aged care homes — a star rating out of five, with four component ratings — but those are published separately on My Aged Care and are not in this list, so no rating is shown on these pages. What is shown is the Government's record of the home: its residential places, its approved provider, how that provider is owned, and the Australian Government funding it received.
