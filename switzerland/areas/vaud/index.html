Nursing homes in Vaud

129 nursing homes on Federal Office of Public Health's register. Register as published 8 April 2026.

Switzerland's nursing homes are licensed and supervised by the 26 cantons, not by the Confederation, and no national rating is published. What is federal is the statistics: the Federal Statistical Office collects a return from every institution each year, and the Federal Office of Public Health publishes operating key figures from it. The figures on this page are that return for the year shown, not a judgement by anyone. Accommodation charges are the home's own charges for board and lodging across the year divided by its accommodation days, so they are an average of what residents were charged and not a price list; residents also pay a share of the care costs, capped by federal law, with health insurance and the canton meeting the rest.
